## ITIL 4 Foundation — Presentation

### Open the slides

- **Option 1 (simplest)**: open `itil4-foundation-presentation/index.html` in your browser.
- **Option 2 (local server)**:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open `http://localhost:8000/itil4-foundation-presentation/index.html`.

### Presenter controls

- **next / prev**: `→` / `←` (or `Space` for next)
- **toggle speaker notes**: `n`
- **blackout screen**: `b`
- **outline (jump to slide)**: `o`
- **go to slide number**: `g`
- **help slide**: `?`

### Export to PDF

Use your browser print dialog:

- **Print**: `Ctrl/Cmd + P`
- Choose **Save to PDF**
